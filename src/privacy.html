Pursuit of Happiness (POH) Community Portal

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Pursuit of Happiness (POH) Apartment Owners Association ("we", "the Association") collects and uses information in the POH Community Portal ("the Portal"), available as a website and mobile application. The Portal is a private tool for residents and the managing committee of our apartment community.

1. Who can use the Portal

Access is restricted to residents (owners and tenants) and committee members who have been approved by a POH administrator. You sign in with your Google account; we do not create separate passwords.

2. Information we collect

  • Account & profile: your name, email address and profile photo from Google Sign-In; your flat number and role (resident, SPOC, committee, admin).
  • Directory information: owner/tenant names, flat and floor, phone numbers, occupancy status, parking and vehicle details — as maintained by the Association for community management.
  • Community activity: event contributions and expenses you record, amenity/move requests, feedback and ratings you submit, and related audit entries.

3. How we use it

  • To operate the community directory, events, contributions/expenses, notices and amenity features.
  • To identify you, apply your role-based permissions, and keep an audit trail of committee actions.
  • To send community communications (e.g., invitations and event updates) related to Association activities.

4. How information is shared

Directory and community information is visible only to approved Portal users. Private contact details are shown in full only to committee/administrators; other residents see a masked view. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

5. Service providers

The Portal runs on Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Hosting and Storage). Data is processed and stored on Google Cloud infrastructure under Google's terms. Sign-in is handled by Google Sign-In.

6. Data retention

We keep community and account records for as long as you are an approved resident/member and as needed for Association record-keeping. You may ask us to remove your access; some records (e.g., historical financial entries and audit logs) may be retained for the Association's accounting and governance.

7. Your choices

You can request to view, correct or delete your personal information, or to revoke your access, by contacting us (below). You can also revoke the Portal's access to your Google account from your Google Account settings.

8. Children

The Portal is intended for adult residents and committee members and is not directed at children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time; the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
