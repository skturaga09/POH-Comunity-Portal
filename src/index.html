Community Guidelines & Undertaking
  1. Shifting activities shall be carried out only during the approved timings communicated by the Association/Builder.
  2. Common areas including corridors, lifts, staircases, lobby, parking areas, and entrances shall not be damaged during shifting.
  3. Any damage caused to common property by movers, residents, or vendors shall be repaired or compensated by me.
  4. Construction debris, packing materials, cartons, thermocol, plastics, or any waste generated during shifting shall be removed immediately and shall not be left in common areas.
  5. Lift capacity limits shall be strictly followed. Overloading lifts is prohibited.
  6. Children shall not use service lifts during shifting activities.
  7. Residents shall ensure that noise is kept to a minimum and no inconvenience is caused to other residents.
  8. No materials shall be stored permanently in corridors, staircases, fire exits, or common areas.
  9. Parking shall be done only in the designated parking space allotted to the flat.
  10. Pets, if any, shall always be under the owner's supervision and community rules regarding pets shall be followed.
  11. All maintenance charges, deposits, and other dues applicable to the flat shall be complied with as per community guidelines.
  12. I shall comply with all safety, security, waste segregation, parking, and community rules communicated by the Association from time to time.
  13. I understand that any violation of community rules may result in action by the Association as per applicable bylaws.

How it will work

From complaint to resolution

1 · RaiseReport an issue with your flat number, category, and a photo.
2 · Auto-assignRouted to the right technician — electrician, plumber, lift, and more.
3 · TrackFollow live status with a ticket number and full timeline.
4 · ResolveThe technician completes the work and closes the ticket.
5 · RateShare feedback on the service once your issue is resolved.
